Biography

Sushant Singh Rajput (1986-2020) was a talented Indian actor who left an indelible mark on Bollywood with his charismatic performances. Known for his roles in films like "MS Dhoni: The Untold Story", "Chhichhore", and "Kai Po Che!", Sushant's journey was one of passion, dedication, and brilliance.
